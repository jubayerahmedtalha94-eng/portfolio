About me

Who am I?

I'm Jubayer Ahmed, a third-year Chemical Engineering student at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), ranked 3rd in my class with a CGPA of 3.91.

My research is on adsorption-based wastewater treatment. I prepare and activate biochar and other biomass-derived adsorbents, characterize them, model how they capture pollutants, and optimize the process. I work in the Biopolymer & Sustainable Environment Research Lab with BCSIR Rajshahi.

Outside the lab I lead. I'm Production Secretary of the Society of Process Engineers (SPER), Campus Lead for Deli Bangladesh (ACI), and a campus ambassador for Unbolt Academy and DREAM Network.

  • Degree: B.Sc. Chemical Engineering
  • University: RUET, Rajshahi
  • Graduating: 2027
  • Lab: BSER Lab × BCSIR
  • Languages: Bangla, English
